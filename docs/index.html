Does this assignment still show you what a student can do — if they used AI to make it?

Learning Signal Check walks your assessment through five questions, stops at the first one that fails, and tells you the one thing to change. Sometimes the answer is "nothing." It is not a cheating detector.

Try it in sixty seconds

  1. Copy the prompt. One tap. It's about 3,700 words written for the assistant, not for you.
  2. Paste it into any chat assistant — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot. The free tier is fine.
  3. Describe one assessment you're worried about, in your own words.

Then tell us what happened

You're the test. Wrong answers are the most useful kind. Three questions, two minutes:

  1. What did you test? Two sentences, no student names.
  2. What did it tell you — and, in your own words, what's the one thing to do?
  3. Was it right, and would you actually do it? What should it have asked about your course?
