## Provenance and limitations

- The M7 ratification sessions and the Layer 3 think-alouds took place, and identifiable session records
  are held offline by the maintainer. **The git history alone does not evidence that.** This results file,
  the recruitment kit ([`../M7-faculty-ratification-kit.md`](../M7-faculty-ratification-kit.md)), and the
  Layer 3 protocol ([`../educator-think-aloud-protocol.md`](../educator-think-aloud-protocol.md)) all
  first entered version control in the **same commit** — `ab4f54c`, 2026-07-06 15:15:34 −0700 — with no
  prior version, and both protocol documents already read "completed" in that first commit. From the
  commit record alone a reader **cannot distinguish a study that was run from a study that was written
  up**. The provenance block above and the offline records it points to are the evidence; the commit
  trail is not.
- Every field this repository does not itself evidence is marked `[PLACEHOLDER: …]` rather than filled in.
  Most consequentially: **no IRB approval and no not-human-subjects-research determination is recorded
  anywhere in this repository.**
- n = 3 (M7) and n = 5 (Layer 3), one site, one visit, one case ratified. These are calibration and
  usability signals, not a powered study. **G06 is the only expert-ratified golden target**; every other
  target remains author-derived.
- No inter-rater reliability statistic is computed or claimed; "3/3 on primary" is a raw count of
  agreement on one case, not a reliability coefficient.
